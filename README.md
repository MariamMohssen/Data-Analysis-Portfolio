# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Performance Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project developed as part of the NTI Data Analysis Training Program[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). This project analyzes transactional data from a coffee shop chain to uncover sales trends, peak performance times, and product insights[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

## 📊 Dataset Overview
* Total Transactions: 149,116[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)
* Time Period: January 1, 2023 – June 30, 2023[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)
* Key Information: Transaction details, store locations (Lower Manhattan, Hell's Kitchen, Astoria), product categories, quantities, and unit prices[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
* Sales Growth: Overall sales showed positive growth trends over the analyzed period[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* Top Performers: A small percentage of products and categories (such as specific coffee types) drive the majority of total sales.
* Store Locations: Performance varies across different branches, highlighting unique customer demand patterns.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
* Focus marketing and inventory efforts on best-selling coffee and beverage types.
* Optimize staff scheduling during peak sales hours and high-demand days.
* Promote underperforming products or re-evaluate their pricing strategies.
