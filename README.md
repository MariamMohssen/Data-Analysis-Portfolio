# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Performance Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project developed as part of the NTI Data Analysis Training Program. This project analyzes transactional data from a coffee shop chain to uncover sales trends, peak performance times, and product insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* Python & Pandas – For data cleaning, validation, handling missing/duplicate values, and engineering new metrics (Sales = Quantity × Price).
* Power BI – For interactive dashboard creation, data modeling, and visual storytelling.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
* Total Transactions: 149,116
* Time Period: January 1, 2023 – June 30, 2023
* Key Information: Transaction details, store locations (Lower Manhattan, Hell's Kitchen, Astoria), product categories, quantities, and unit prices.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
* Sales Growth: Overall sales showed positive growth trends over the analyzed period.
* Top Performers: A small percentage of products and categories (such as specific coffee types) drive the majority of total sales.
* Store Locations: Performance varies across different branches, highlighting unique customer demand patterns.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
* Focus marketing and inventory efforts on best-selling coffee and beverage types.
* Optimize staff scheduling during peak sales hours and high-demand days.
* Promote underperforming products or re-evaluate their pricing strategies.
